Why you are keeping this

Two reasons, and neither of them is surveillance. The first is that estimating your own work is a professional skill you will be asked to exercise constantly, and the only way to get better at it is to compare what you predicted against what actually happened. The second is that the reflection section of your report needs material, and a log that shows discovery took half the time you expected while evaluation took twice as long gives you something genuinely worth writing about.

Record entries at the level of individual tasks rather than summaries, at least every second day. Record the hours you actually worked, including the ones that produced nothing useful. A task that took four hours and ended in a dead end is a real cost and belongs here.
